## RoboTUM

Forrest is built by [RoboTUM](https://www.robotum.info/), a student robotics team at the Technical University of Munich.

## My role

I work on RL policy training: I ported the robot and its leg tendons to MuJoCo and train running policies with PPO, 16 384 robots in parallel on one GPU.
