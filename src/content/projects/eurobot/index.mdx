## Problem

Eurobot is an autonomous robotics competition: robots score points on a
3000 × 2000 mm field in a 100-second match, with no human input and an
opponent moving on the same table. That demands reliable motion, safe
obstacle handling and a strategy that adapts to what is actually on the
field.

## Architecture

Two-layer stack: real-time control written from scratch on the STM32H7, perception and strategy in ROS 2 on a Raspberry Pi 5.
