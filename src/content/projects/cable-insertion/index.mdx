## Problem

Fully learned AI policies are often non-deterministic: the same scene can produce different outcomes from run to run. That’s risky in industrial assembly, especially with small, fragile parts like fibre-optic connectors, which need sub-millimetre alignment and break if forced.

## Architecture

The system combines camera-based pose estimation with finely tuned impedance control. Perception gets the gripper close, using either stereo reconstruction (YOLO segmentation + SVD triangulation) or direct 3D estimation. Compliant control with force feedback and a spiral search then finds the port despite small estimation errors. The result is predictable behaviour that transferred from a simulated UR5e to a real xArm7.

## My contribution

Led the team and developed the perception pipeline and the impedance-controlled insertion policy.

## Results

Direct 3D estimation proved more robust than stereo reconstruction.
