## Problem

People adjust their thermostat by hand all day. The goal was a device that
learns those preferences and sets the temperature itself, running on a
low-cost microcontroller without a constant cloud connection.

## Architecture

The thermostat has three modes:

- **Away**: holds an energy-saving setpoint while nobody is home.
- **Manual**: the user sets the temperature directly. Every manual
  adjustment is logged as training data.
- **Auto**: a trained neural network runs on the ESP32 (TensorFlow Lite)
  and predicts the setpoint the user would choose.

## My contribution

Trained and compared regression and neural network models, selected the
best-performing one, and deployed it on the ESP32 with TensorFlow Lite.
